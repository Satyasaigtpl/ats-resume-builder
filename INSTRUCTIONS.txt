ResumeAI Pro - Step-by-Step AI Usage Guide
=============================================

This guide explains how to use the different AI Providers inside your local ResumeAI application.

--------------------------------------------------
Method 1: Using Ollama (100% Free & Local Privacy)
--------------------------------------------------
This method runs the AI completely on your own graphics card/processor. No resume data ever leaves your computer.

1. Download and install the Ollama desktop app from https://ollama.com.
2. Open your terminal/command prompt and download a model. For example: `ollama run llama3` (this might take a few minutes the first time to download the model).
3. Keep the Ollama app running in the background.
4. Launch your ResumeAI app using `run_app.bat`.
5. In the left sidebar, change the "API Provider" dropdown to "Ollama (Local)".
6. In the box below it, type the exact name of the model you downloaded (e.g., "llama3" or "mistral").
7. Upload your PDFs and click "Start Optimization Process".

--------------------------------------------------
Method 2: Using Groq Llama 3 (Free & Ultra Fast)
--------------------------------------------------
This method uses Groq's cloud servers. It is completely free and incredibly fast (often processing in under 5 seconds).

1. Go to https://console.groq.com/keys and create a free account.
2. Click "Create API Key" and copy the long string of letters and numbers it gives you.
3. Launch your ResumeAI app using `run_app.bat`.
4. In the left sidebar, select "Groq (Llama 3)" from the dropdown menu.
5. Paste your API Key into the password box.
6. Upload your PDFs and click "Start Optimization Process".

--------------------------------------------------
Method 3: Using Gemini Pro (Free Google AI)
--------------------------------------------------
This method uses Google's powerful Gemini 1.5 Pro model. It has a generous free tier perfect for personal use.

1. Go to Google AI Studio at https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey.
2. Sign in with your Google account and click the blue "Create API key" button.
3. Copy the generated API key.
4. Launch your ResumeAI app using `run_app.bat`.
5. In the left sidebar, select "Gemini (Google)" from the dropdown menu.
6. Paste your API Key into the password box.
7. Upload your PDFs and click "Start Optimization Process".

--------------------------------------------------
General Usage Tips for Best Results:
--------------------------------------------------
- Master Documents: Ensure your Master Resume and Cover Letter PDFs are clean (text-selectable, not scanned images).
- Target Language: If you select "German (Deutsch)", the AI will automatically translate your entire resume while optimizing it for the job description!
- Always review the final PDF text, as AI models sometimes hallucinate slight details.
